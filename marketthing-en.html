Your subscription gives you the whole department. On top of it, you pay only for the actual thinking the AI does - measured in tokens, the same way the engine behind it bills. No padding, no idle-seat fees. Here is exactly how it works.

01

What a token is

A token is a small chunk of text - roughly a few characters - that the AI reads and writes. A short reel costs a little. A researched, fact-checked article costs more. You pay for what was produced, nothing else.

02

Metered live, per agent

Every conversation is counted in real time and attributed to the agent that did the work. The usage dashboard shows tokens and cost per agent, per day, so you always know where it went.

03

What most founders spend

A solo brand posting daily content typically runs about $30 to $90 a month in tokens on top of the $199. Quiet weeks cost less. A big launch month costs a bit more. It scales with your activity, not a fixed bracket.

04

You set the ceiling

Set a monthly token budget and the console warns you before you reach it. No bill-shock, no surprise invoice - you decide the cap, you see it fill up.
